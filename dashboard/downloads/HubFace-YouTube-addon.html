HubFace YouTube add-on

Makes videos you click in the YouTube pop-up play inside HubFace.

  1. Download the add-on and unzip it to a folder you'll keep (e.g. Documents\HubFace-YouTube-addon).
  2. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions (type it in the address bar).
  3. Turn on Developer mode (top-right switch).
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
  5. Reload HubFace. It only acts in the HubFace YouTube pop-up.

To remove it: chrome://extensions → HubFace YouTube pop-up loader → Remove.
